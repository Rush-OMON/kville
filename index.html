About K-ville

A growing food and bakery-focused company in Edo State.

K-ville brings together creativity, training and dependable product supply. Through K-ville Cakes, the brand has built a reputation for detailed custom cakes and practical training since 2015. Through K-ville Distribution, the company supports bakeries with consistent access to quality ingredients and trusted bakery brands.

K-ville Cakes

Luxury cakes, sugar craft and hands-on baking training.

K-ville Cakes, also known as @k_villecakes, is a custom bakery business founded in 2015 in Ekpoma, Edo State. The team creates bespoke cakes tailored to personal stories, event themes, cultural celebrations and corporate milestones.

  • Wedding and luxury event cakes
  • Birthday, chieftaincy and themed cakes
  • Inaugural lecture and corporate celebration cakes
  • Baking, decorating and sugar craft classes
